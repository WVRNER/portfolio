Core Foundations & Hands-On Study

I’m currently developing my foundations across Networking, Linux, Cloud Infrastructure, Git, Containers, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, Automation, and Monitoring. I’m learning through structured study and hands-on labs, with an emphasis on understanding how the pieces connect rather than simply learning individual tools.
